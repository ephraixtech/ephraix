FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Minimal, rounded, and clean so the visitor can move forward without friction.

Are the smart contracts fully audited?

Yes. All smart contracts we deliver are formally audited. We write complete test coverage suites (Foundry/Hardhat) and coordinate with leading audit companies like CertiK, Hacken, or Halborn.

How do you achieve optimal gas efficiency in the contracts?

We write highly optimized Solidity, utilizing custom storage packing, custom Yul assembly codes, and standard gas-optimized presets like ERC721A for batch NFT minting.

Do you support cross-chain token deployments?

Yes. We engineer cross-chain tokens using standards like LayerZero's Omnichain Fungible Token (OFT) and Chainlink's CCIP, allowing users to bridge tokens seamlessly without wrapped-contract vulnerability risks.

How do you handle token vesting schedules for teams and advisors?

We develop audited, non-custodial vesting smart contracts that lock tokens on-chain and release them linearly or in cliff segments according to your schedule, complete with owner-revoke capabilities.

What token standards do you build?

We support all standard token architectures including ERC-20 (fungible), ERC-721/721A/721x (non-fungible), ERC-1155 (multi-token), SPL (Solana standard), and BEP-20 (BNB standard).

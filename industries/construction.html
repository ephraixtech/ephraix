Can your systems integrate with existing site cameras and IoT sensors?

Yes. We build ingestion pipelines that connect to existing camera networks, drones, and IoT sensors without requiring a hardware refresh.

How does blockchain materials tracking actually work on-site?

Suppliers and site teams log verified checkpoints (dispatch, delivery, installation) to an immutable ledger, giving you a tamper-proof chain of custody.

Can smart contracts really automate subcontractor payments?

Yes, milestone-based escrow releases funds automatically once verified progress conditions are met, reducing disputes and payment delays.

Do you support multi-site project portfolios?

Our architecture is designed to scale from a single site to a portfolio of active projects with centralized reporting.

How do you handle safety compliance across regions?

We encode region-specific safety and reporting requirements directly into the compliance checklist engine.
